Workforce Intelligence Dashboard - Power BI | DAX | Power Query | HR Analytics

Project Overview
Developed an interactive Workforce Intelligence Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee headcount, hiring activity, workforce separations, attrition, and employee engagement.

The dashboard helps HR leaders monitor workforce trends, identify potential retention concerns, and make data-informed decisions.

Tools & Technologies
- Power BI: Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- DAX: KPI calculations and rolling 12-month metrics
- Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation
- Data Modeling: Relationships between employee snapshots, workforce events, and dimension tables

Dashboard Features
- Historical workforce headcount analysis
- Monthly hiring and separation reporting
- Department-level attrition comparisons
- Location-level attrition analysis
- Voluntary versus involuntary separation analysis
- Interactive department, location, year, and reporting-month filters
- Dynamic reporting-period subtitle

Key Business Insights
- Client Services recorded the highest departmental attrition rate at 13.13%, exceeding the company benchmark of 9.46%.
- Tampa had the highest location-level attrition rate at 12.68%.
- Of the 19 September separations, 16 were voluntary, and 3 were involuntary.
- The workforce recorded positive net movement of seven employees in September 2026.

These findings can help HR teams prioritize further investigation of workforce retention and employee experience.

Technical Approach
1. Imported and transformed workforce data using Power Query.
2. Created a relational data model connecting department, location, job level, and monthly workforce data.
3. Developed DAX measures for headcount, hiring, separations, and trailing 12-month attrition.
4. Designed executive-facing visualizations with interactive filters.
5. Troubleshot filter context and date relationships to support historical trend reporting.
